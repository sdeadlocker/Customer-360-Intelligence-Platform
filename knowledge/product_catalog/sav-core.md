# Core Savings — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** SAV-CORE
**Business Group:** Deposits

## Overview

Core Savings (SAV-CORE) is the entry-level savings account used to establish a savings habit and to serve as overdraft protection for linked checking. It is the most commonly opened second product for new checking customers and functions as a stepping stone toward higher-yield deposit products.

## Features

- Automatic transfer scheduling for recurring savings goals.
- Optional linkage as overdraft protection for Everyday Checking (DDA-EVERYDAY).
- Round-up transfers from linked debit card purchases.
- Named savings goals within the mobile app.

## Eligibility

- Account owner must be 18 years or older (custodial option for minors).
- Valid government-issued identification and verified U.S. address.
- Minimum opening deposit of $25.
- Successful identity verification.

## Rates and Fees

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Monthly maintenance fee | $5.00 | Waived with $300 minimum daily balance |
| Minimum opening deposit | $25.00 | One-time |
| Excess withdrawal fee | $10.00 | After 6 withdrawals per cycle |
| Interest rate (APY) | 0.10% | Standard tier |

## Cross-Sell Fit

SAV-CORE holders accumulating balances above $10,000 should be introduced to High Yield Savings (SAV-HIYIELD) or a 12 Month Certificate (CD-12M). Pair with DDA-EVERYDAY for overdraft protection.

## Disclosures

APY is variable and subject to change. Federal transfer limits may apply depending on account configuration. See the deposit account policy.

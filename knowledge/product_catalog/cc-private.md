# Private Client Card — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** CC-PRIVATE
**Business Group:** Cards

## Overview

The Private Client Card (CC-PRIVATE) is an invitation-oriented premium card reserved for private banking and mass-affluent relationships. It offers concierge service, elevated rewards, and relationship-based pricing. CC-PRIVATE deepens affluent relationships and is typically bundled with managed investments and Premier Checking.

## Features

- Dedicated concierge and travel desk.
- Relationship-based rewards multipliers.
- Comprehensive travel and purchase protections.
- Complimentary authorized user cards.

## Eligibility

- Existing Premier Checking (DDA-PREMIER) or managed investment relationship.
- Minimum qualifying credit score of 740.
- Combined relationship balances of $100,000 or more.
- Successful identity verification.

## Rates and Fees

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Annual fee | $250 | Waived at $250k relationship |
| Purchase APR | 18.99%–24.99% | Variable, relationship-priced |
| Rewards rate | 2x–4x points | Relationship tiers |

## Cross-Sell Fit

CC-PRIVATE holders should have INV-MANAGED and DDA-PREMIER. Review trust and estate needs (INV-TRUST) and HELOC-VAR for liquidity. Use the affluent cross-sell playbook.

## Disclosures

APR is variable and relationship-priced. Benefits subject to guides. See required consumer credit disclosures.

# 30 Year Fixed Mortgage — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** MTG-30F
**Business Group:** Lending

## Overview

The 30 Year Fixed Mortgage (MTG-30F) is the cornerstone home-purchase product, offering predictable monthly principal-and-interest payments over a 360-month term. It is the most common first mortgage and a primary driver of deposit and wealth cross-sell for homebuying households.

## Features

- Fixed interest rate for the entire 30-year term.
- Available for purchase and rate-and-term refinance.
- Optional rate lock for 30, 45, or 60 days.
- Escrow for taxes and insurance available.

## Eligibility

- Minimum qualifying credit score of 620.
- Maximum debt-to-income ratio of 45% (exceptions per lending policy).
- Documented, verifiable income and assets.
- Property must meet appraisal and occupancy requirements.
- Down payment of at least 3% for qualified first-time buyers.

## Cross-Sell Fit

MTG-30F borrowers are candidates for Home Equity Line (HELOC-VAR) after building equity, and for deposit consolidation into Premier Checking (DDA-PREMIER). Use the first home playbook for first-time buyers and the refinance playbook for existing homeowners.

## Disclosures

APR reflects the cost of credit as a yearly rate and depends on points, fees, and rate. See required TILA lending disclosures and the lending policy.

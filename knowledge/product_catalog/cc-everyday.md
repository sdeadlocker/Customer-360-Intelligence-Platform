# Everyday Rewards Card — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** CC-EVERYDAY
**Business Group:** Cards

## Overview

The Everyday Rewards Card (CC-EVERYDAY) is a no-annual-fee rewards credit card designed as the entry card for retail customers. It pairs simple flat-rate rewards with broad acceptance and is the most common first card for new-to-credit and new-to-bank customers.

## Features

- Flat-rate rewards on all purchases.
- No annual fee.
- Contactless payments and digital wallet support.
- Real-time transaction alerts and spend controls.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be 18 years or older.
- Minimum qualifying credit score of 660.
- Documented, verifiable income.
- Successful identity verification.

## Rates and Fees

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Annual fee | $0 | — |
| Purchase APR | 19.99%–27.99% | Variable, risk-based |
| Rewards rate | 1.5% | All purchases |
| Late payment fee | Up to $40 | — |
| Foreign transaction fee | 3% | Per transaction |

## Cross-Sell Fit

CC-EVERYDAY holders who travel are candidates for the Travel Elite Card (CC-TRAVEL); heavy spenders for the Cashback Plus Card (CC-CASHBACK). Pair with Everyday Checking (DDA-EVERYDAY) for autopay.

## Disclosures

APR is variable and risk-based. Rewards terms are subject to the rewards program agreement. See required consumer credit disclosures.

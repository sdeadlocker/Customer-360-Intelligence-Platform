# Everyday Checking — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** DDA-EVERYDAY
**Business Group:** Deposits

## Overview

Everyday Checking (DDA-EVERYDAY) is the flagship transactional deposit account for retail customers who need reliable day-to-day money management. The account pairs a no-frills fee structure with broad digital access, making it the default recommendation for new-to-bank consumers and students transitioning to independent banking. Everyday Checking is designed to be the anchor relationship product from which deposit and lending cross-sell flows.

## Features

- Unlimited debit card transactions with contactless tap-to-pay.
- Mobile check deposit and same-day peer-to-peer transfers.
- Real-time balance alerts and low-balance push notifications.
- Access to a nationwide surcharge-free ATM network.
- Optional linked overdraft protection from a Core Savings account.

## Eligibility

- Primary applicant must be 18 years or older (16+ with a joint adult owner).
- Valid government-issued identification and a verifiable U.S. address.
- Successful identity verification and deposit-screening review.
- Minimum opening deposit of $25.
- No more than two prior involuntary account closures in the past 24 months.

## Cross-Sell Fit

DDA-EVERYDAY customers with recurring direct deposit are strong candidates for the Everyday Rewards Card (CC-EVERYDAY) and Core Savings (SAV-CORE). Customers maintaining balances above $10,000 should be introduced to High Yield Savings (SAV-HIYIELD) or Money Market Select (MMA-SELECT). Direct-deposit-active customers who inquire about home purchase are a natural referral to the 30 Year Fixed Mortgage (MTG-30F).

## Disclosures

Fees are subject to the account agreement and the fee schedule in effect at the time a fee is assessed. APY is variable and may change after account opening. See the deposit account policy and required consumer disclosures for complete terms.

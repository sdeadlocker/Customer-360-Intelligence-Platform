# Money Market Select — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** MMA-SELECT
**Business Group:** Deposits

## Overview

Money Market Select (MMA-SELECT) blends the liquidity of a savings account with limited check-writing and tiered yield. It suits customers who want higher balances to remain accessible while earning a competitive rate. MMA-SELECT is positioned between High Yield Savings and certificates in the deposit ladder.

## Features

- Limited check-writing and debit access for larger transactions.
- Tiered yield rewarding higher balances.
- Sweep configuration options for business and personal use.
- Same-day transfers to linked checking.

## Eligibility

- Account owner must be 18 years or older.
- Valid government-issued identification and verified U.S. address.
- Minimum opening deposit of $2,500.
- Successful identity verification.

## Rates and Fees

| Balance Tier | APY | Notes |
|--------------|-----|-------|
| $2,500 – $49,999 | 2.90% | Base tier |
| $50,000 – $249,999 | 3.60% | Preferred tier |
| $250,000+ | 4.00% | Premium tier |
| Monthly maintenance fee | $12.00 | Waived at $10,000 balance |
| Excess transaction fee | $12.00 | After 6 per cycle |

## Cross-Sell Fit

MMA-SELECT holders are candidates for certificates (CD-12M, CD-36M) for funds not needed short-term and for INV-MANAGED for long-horizon goals. Business customers should be paired with the Business Operating Account (BUS-CHK).

## Disclosures

APY is variable. Transaction limits may apply. See the deposit account policy.

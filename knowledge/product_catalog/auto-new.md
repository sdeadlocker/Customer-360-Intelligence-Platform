# New Vehicle Loan — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** AUTO-NEW
**Business Group:** Lending

## Overview

The New Vehicle Loan (AUTO-NEW) finances the purchase of new automobiles with competitive fixed rates and flexible terms. It is a high-velocity consumer lending product that supports both direct-to-consumer and dealer-referred originations.

## Features

- Fixed rate financing for new vehicles.
- Terms from 36 to 72 months.
- Rate discount for automatic payment from a linked deposit account.
- Pre-approval available before dealer visit.

## Eligibility

- Minimum qualifying credit score of 620.
- Vehicle model year current or prior year, zero prior owners.
- Maximum loan-to-value of 120% including tax and fees.
- Documented, verifiable income.

## Rates and Fees

| Term | Sample APR | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| 36 months | 6.49% | With autopay discount |
| 60 months | 6.99% | With autopay discount |
| 72 months | 7.49% | With autopay discount |
| Late payment fee | $25 | After grace period |
| Document fee | $75 | One-time |

## Cross-Sell Fit

AUTO-NEW borrowers should have a linked deposit account for the autopay discount and are candidates for the Everyday Rewards Card (CC-EVERYDAY). Refer purchase-intent homebuyers to MTG-30F.

## Disclosures

APR depends on term, credit, and collateral. Autopay discount requires a linked account in good standing. See TILA lending disclosures.

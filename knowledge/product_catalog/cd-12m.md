# 12 Month Certificate — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** CD-12M
**Business Group:** Deposits

## Overview

The 12 Month Certificate (CD-12M) is a fixed-term, fixed-rate deposit for customers willing to lock funds for one year in exchange for a guaranteed yield. It is a core component of a deposit laddering strategy and a common destination for funds customers do not need in the short term.

## Features

- Guaranteed fixed rate for the full 12-month term.
- Automatic renewal at maturity unless instructed otherwise.
- 10-day grace period after maturity to add or withdraw funds.
- Interest paid monthly or at maturity.

## Eligibility

- Account owner must be 18 years or older.
- Valid government-issued identification and verified U.S. address.
- Minimum opening deposit of $1,000.

## Rates and Fees

| Term | APY | Early Withdrawal Penalty |
|------|-----|--------------------------|
| 12 months | 4.15% | 90 days of interest |
| Minimum deposit | $1,000 | — |
| Additional deposits | Not allowed | During term |

## Cross-Sell Fit

CD-12M holders should be introduced to CD-36M for longer horizons and to SAV-HIYIELD for liquid reserves. At maturity, review whether proceeds should ladder into a new certificate or move to managed investments (INV-MANAGED).

## Disclosures

Early withdrawal penalties may reduce principal. Renewal rates reflect the rate in effect at maturity. See the deposit account policy.

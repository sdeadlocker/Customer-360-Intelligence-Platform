# Premier Checking — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** DDA-PREMIER
**Business Group:** Deposits

## Overview

Premier Checking (DDA-PREMIER) is the relationship-tier checking account built for affluent and mass-affluent households that consolidate deposits, investments, and lending. It rewards balance depth with fee waivers, preferential rates, and dedicated service. Premier Checking is the anchor product for the affluent cross-sell motion and is frequently bundled with managed investments and premium cards.

## Features

- Waived fees across most linked deposit accounts when relationship balances qualify.
- Preferred interest rate tiers on savings and money market balances.
- Complimentary domestic wire transfers and cashier's checks.
- Priority phone servicing and a dedicated relationship banker.
- Fee reimbursement for out-of-network ATM usage worldwide.

## Eligibility

- Primary applicant must be 18 years or older.
- Combined qualifying balances of $25,000 across eligible deposit and investment accounts.
- Valid government-issued identification and verified U.S. address.
- Successful identity verification and deposit-screening review.

## Rates and Fees

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Monthly maintenance fee | $30.00 | Waived with $25,000 relationship balance |
| Domestic wire (outgoing) | $0.00 | Included |
| Overdraft item fee | $30.00 | Waived once per statement cycle |
| Out-of-network ATM | $0.00 | Reimbursed |
| Interest rate (APY) | 0.05% | Relationship tier |

## Cross-Sell Fit

DDA-PREMIER holders are prime candidates for INV-MANAGED, the Private Client Card (CC-PRIVATE), and Home Equity Line (HELOC-VAR). Advisors should use the affluent cross-sell playbook to structure a full relationship review.

## Disclosures

Relationship balance requirements are evaluated monthly. APY is variable. See the deposit account policy for qualifying-balance definitions.

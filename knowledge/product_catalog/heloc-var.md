# Home Equity Line — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** HELOC-VAR
**Business Group:** Lending

## Overview

The Home Equity Line (HELOC-VAR) is a revolving, variable-rate line of credit secured by the equity in a borrower's primary residence. It offers flexible access to funds during a draw period followed by a repayment period. HELOC-VAR is a key product for homeowners seeking to fund renovations, education, or consolidation at a lower rate than unsecured credit.

## Features

- Revolving credit with a multi-year draw period.
- Variable rate indexed to a published benchmark plus a margin.
- Interest-only payment option during the draw period.
- Access via checks, transfers, or a linked card.

## Eligibility

- Owner-occupied primary residence with sufficient equity.
- Minimum qualifying credit score of 680.
- Combined loan-to-value not exceeding 85%.
- Maximum debt-to-income ratio of 43%.
- Documented, verifiable income.

## Cross-Sell Fit

HELOC-VAR is a natural companion to MTG-30F and MTG-15F borrowers who have accumulated equity. Affluent homeowners are candidates for INV-MANAGED and Private Client Card (CC-PRIVATE).

## Disclosures

Variable rates may increase, raising payments. Your home secures the line and is at risk in default. See TILA lending disclosures and the lending policy.

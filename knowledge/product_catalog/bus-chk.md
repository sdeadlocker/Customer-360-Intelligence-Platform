# Business Operating Account — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** BUS-CHK
**Business Group:** Business

## Overview

The Business Operating Account (BUS-CHK) is the primary transactional deposit account for small and mid-sized businesses. It supports payroll, vendor payments, and cash management while serving as the anchor for business lending and merchant services cross-sell.

## Features

- Multiple user entitlements with role-based permissions.
- ACH origination and outbound wire capability.
- Integration with common accounting software.
- Optional sweep to Money Market Select (MMA-SELECT).

## Eligibility

- Registered business entity with valid tax identification number.
- Beneficial ownership certification for all owners of 25% or more.
- Authorized signer with valid government-issued identification.
- Successful business identity verification and KYC review.

## Rates and Fees

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Monthly maintenance fee | $20.00 | Waived at $5,000 average balance |
| Transactions included | 250/month | $0.40 each thereafter |
| Cash deposit processing | $0.30 per $100 | Over $10,000/month |
| Outgoing wire (domestic) | $25.00 | Per wire |

## Cross-Sell Fit

BUS-CHK holders are candidates for the Business Term Loan (BUS-TERM), Business Expense Card (CC-BUSINESS), and MMA-SELECT sweeps. Use the business formation playbook for new entities.

## Disclosures

Beneficial ownership must be recertified on material changes. See the KYC onboarding policy.

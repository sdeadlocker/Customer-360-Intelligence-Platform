# Personal Loan — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** PL-UNSEC
**Business Group:** Lending

## Overview

The Personal Loan (PL-UNSEC) is an unsecured fixed-rate installment loan for debt consolidation, major purchases, or unexpected expenses. Because it is unsecured, pricing is risk-based and eligibility is tighter than for collateralized products.

## Features

- Fixed rate and fixed monthly payment.
- Terms from 24 to 60 months.
- No collateral required.
- Funds disbursed to a linked deposit account, often same day.

## Eligibility

- Minimum qualifying credit score of 660.
- Maximum debt-to-income ratio of 40%.
- Documented, verifiable income.
- No unresolved delinquencies within the past 12 months.

## Rates and Fees

| Credit Tier | Sample APR | Notes |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| Excellent (740+) | 9.99% | Best rate |
| Good (700–739) | 13.99% | — |
| Fair (660–699) | 18.99% | — |
| Origination fee | 1–5% | Deducted from proceeds |
| Late payment fee | $25 | After grace period |

## Cross-Sell Fit

PL-UNSEC borrowers consolidating debt may benefit from the Cashback Plus Card (CC-CASHBACK) once balances are managed. Homeowners with equity should be compared against HELOC-VAR, which may offer a lower rate.

## Disclosures

Rates are risk-based. Origination fees reduce the amount disbursed. See TILA lending disclosures and the lending policy.

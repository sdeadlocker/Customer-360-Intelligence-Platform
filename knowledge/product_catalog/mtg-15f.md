# 15 Year Fixed Mortgage — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** MTG-15F
**Business Group:** Lending

## Overview

The 15 Year Fixed Mortgage (MTG-15F) offers a lower rate than the 30-year term in exchange for higher monthly payments and faster equity building. It suits financially established borrowers focused on paying off their home sooner and minimizing total interest.

## Features

- Fixed rate for the full 15-year term.
- Faster principal amortization and equity accumulation.
- Available for purchase and refinance.
- Escrow for taxes and insurance available.

## Eligibility

- Minimum qualifying credit score of 640.
- Maximum debt-to-income ratio of 43%.
- Documented, verifiable income and assets.
- Property must meet appraisal and occupancy requirements.

## Rates and Fees

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Sample rate (APR) | 5.95% | Illustrative |
| Origination fee | 1.00% | Of loan amount |
| Appraisal fee | $550 | Third-party |
| Late payment fee | 5% of P&I | After grace period |

## Cross-Sell Fit

MTG-15F borrowers frequently qualify for HELOC-VAR and are strong candidates for managed investments (INV-MANAGED) given their savings capacity. Use the refinance playbook when converting from a 30-year term.

## Disclosures

APR depends on points and fees. See required TILA lending disclosures and the lending policy.

# High Yield Savings — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** SAV-HIYIELD
**Business Group:** Deposits

## Overview

High Yield Savings (SAV-HIYIELD) is a premium liquid savings account offering a competitive annual percentage yield in exchange for higher minimum balances. It targets customers with meaningful cash reserves who want liquidity plus yield. SAV-HIYIELD is a key deposit-gathering product and a frequent destination for maturing certificate proceeds.

## Features

- Tiered interest that increases with balance depth.
- Same-day internal transfers to linked checking.
- No transaction fees for internal transfers.
- Rate-tracking alerts when tier thresholds are crossed.

## Eligibility

- Account owner must be 18 years or older.
- Valid government-issued identification and verified U.S. address.
- Minimum opening deposit of $1,000.
- Successful identity verification.

## Rates and Fees

| Balance Tier | APY | Monthly Fee |
|--------------|-----|-------------|
| $1,000 – $24,999 | 3.25% | $10.00 (waived at $10k) |
| $25,000 – $99,999 | 3.75% | $0.00 |
| $100,000+ | 4.10% | $0.00 |
| Excess withdrawal fee | — | $10.00 after 6/cycle |

## Cross-Sell Fit

SAV-HIYIELD customers are strong candidates for Money Market Select (MMA-SELECT), certificates (CD-12M, CD-36M), and managed investments (INV-MANAGED) once emergency reserves are established. Pair with Premier Checking (DDA-PREMIER) for relationship fee waivers.

## Disclosures

APY is variable and may change at any time after opening. Promotional bonuses, when offered, are governed by separate offer terms. See the deposit account policy and required consumer disclosures.

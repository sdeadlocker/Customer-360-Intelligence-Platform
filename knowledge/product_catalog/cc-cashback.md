# Cashback Plus Card — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** CC-CASHBACK
**Business Group:** Cards

## Overview

The Cashback Plus Card (CC-CASHBACK) is a tiered cash-rewards credit card for customers who concentrate spend in everyday categories such as groceries, fuel, and dining. It is positioned above the Everyday Rewards Card for customers who can maximize category bonuses.

## Features

- Elevated cash back in rotating and fixed bonus categories.
- No annual fee.
- Redeem as statement credit or deposit to a linked account.
- Introductory APR on purchases for new accounts (see offer terms).

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be 18 years or older.
- Minimum qualifying credit score of 680.
- Documented, verifiable income.
- Successful identity verification.

## Rates and Fees

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Annual fee | $0 | — |
| Purchase APR | 18.99%–26.99% | Variable, risk-based |
| Grocery/fuel rewards | 3% | Up to quarterly cap |
| Base rewards | 1% | All other purchases |
| Foreign transaction fee | 3% | Per transaction |

## Cross-Sell Fit

CC-CASHBACK holders with strong deposit relationships are candidates for the Travel Elite Card (CC-TRAVEL) or Private Client Card (CC-PRIVATE). Redemption to a linked SAV-CORE reinforces the savings habit.

## Disclosures

APR is variable and risk-based. Introductory offers are governed by separate offer terms. Category caps apply. See required consumer credit disclosures.

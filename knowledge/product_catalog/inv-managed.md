# Managed Investment Portfolio — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** INV-MANAGED
**Business Group:** Wealth

## Overview

The Managed Investment Portfolio (INV-MANAGED) is a discretionary, professionally managed investment account aligned to a client's goals, risk tolerance, and time horizon. It is the core wealth product for affluent relationships and the primary vehicle for long-horizon savings beyond deposits.

## Features

- Discretionary portfolio management by licensed advisors.
- Goal-based allocation and periodic rebalancing.
- Tax-aware strategies and consolidated reporting.
- Integration with retirement (INV-IRA) and education (INV-529) accounts.

## Eligibility

- Account holder must be 18 years or older.
- Minimum investment of $50,000.
- Completed suitability and risk-tolerance assessment.
- Successful identity verification.

## Cross-Sell Fit

INV-MANAGED clients should hold Premier Checking (DDA-PREMIER) and are candidates for the Private Client Card (CC-PRIVATE), trust services (INV-TRUST), and HELOC-VAR for liquidity. Use the retirement planning and affluent cross-sell playbooks.

## Disclosures

Investments are not deposits, are not FDIC insured, and may lose value. Advisory fees are disclosed in the advisory agreement. Recommendations are subject to suitability requirements. See advice suitability compliance guidance.

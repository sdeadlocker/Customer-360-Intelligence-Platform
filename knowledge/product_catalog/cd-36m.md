# 36 Month Certificate — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** CD-36M
**Business Group:** Deposits

## Overview

The 36 Month Certificate (CD-36M) is a three-year fixed-rate deposit for customers with a longer savings horizon who want to lock in a rate. It anchors the long end of a deposit ladder and is a common recommendation when rate expectations are uncertain.

## Features

- Guaranteed fixed rate for the full 36-month term.
- Automatic renewal at maturity unless instructed otherwise.
- Interest paid monthly, quarterly, or at maturity.
- 10-day grace period after maturity.

## Eligibility

- Account owner must be 18 years or older.
- Valid government-issued identification and verified U.S. address.
- Minimum opening deposit of $1,000.

## Rates and Fees

| Term | APY | Early Withdrawal Penalty |
|------|-----|--------------------------|
| 36 months | 4.35% | 180 days of interest |
| Minimum deposit | $1,000 | — |
| Additional deposits | Not allowed | During term |

## Cross-Sell Fit

CD-36M complements shorter certificates (CD-12M) in a laddering strategy. At maturity, evaluate managed investments (INV-MANAGED) for customers with a long horizon.

## Disclosures

Early withdrawal penalties may reduce principal. See the deposit account policy.

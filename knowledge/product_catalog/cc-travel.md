# Travel Elite Card — Product Sheet

**Product Code:** CC-TRAVEL
**Business Group:** Cards

## Overview

The Travel Elite Card (CC-TRAVEL) is a premium travel rewards card for frequent travelers who value points, lounge access, and travel protections. It carries an annual fee offset by travel credits and elevated earn rates. CC-TRAVEL is a strong affluent cross-sell anchor.

## Features

- Elevated points on travel and dining.
- Annual travel statement credit and lounge access.
- No foreign transaction fees.
- Trip protection and rental car coverage.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be 18 years or older.
- Minimum qualifying credit score of 720.
- Documented, verifiable income.
- Successful identity verification.

## Rates and Fees

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Annual fee | $95 | Offset by travel credit |
| Purchase APR | 20.99%–28.99% | Variable, risk-based |
| Travel/dining rewards | 3x points | — |
| Base rewards | 1x points | All other purchases |
| Foreign transaction fee | $0 | — |

## Cross-Sell Fit

CC-TRAVEL holders are candidates for the Private Client Card (CC-PRIVATE) and Premier Checking (DDA-PREMIER). Use the affluent cross-sell playbook.

## Disclosures

APR is variable and risk-based. Travel benefits are subject to benefit guides. Sign-up bonuses are governed by separate offer terms. See required consumer credit disclosures.

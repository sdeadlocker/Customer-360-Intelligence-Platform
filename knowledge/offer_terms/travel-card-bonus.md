# Travel Elite Card Sign-Up Bonus Offer

**Product:** Travel Elite Card (CC-TRAVEL)

## Overview

This document sets out the terms and conditions of the sign-up points bonus for new Travel Elite Card (CC-TRAVEL) accounts.

## Offer Summary

New Travel Elite Card accounts earn a points bonus after meeting a minimum spend threshold in the first 90 days.

## Effective Window

- Offer available for applications received from 2025-01-15 through 2025-11-30.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be approved for a new CC-TRAVEL account.
- Applicant must not have received a CC-TRAVEL bonus in the prior 24 months.
- Minimum spend of $4,000 within the first 90 days.

## Terms Table

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Minimum spend | $4,000 in 90 days |
| Bonus points | 60,000 points |
| Estimated value | Varies by redemption |
| Annual fee | $95, not waived |

## Disclosures

Points are subject to the rewards program agreement. Balance transfers and cash advances do not count toward minimum spend. See required consumer credit disclosures.

# High Yield Savings Bonus Offer

**Product:** High Yield Savings (SAV-HIYIELD)

## Overview

This document sets out the terms and conditions of the new-money cash bonus for opening a High Yield Savings (SAV-HIYIELD) account.

## Offer Summary

Open a new SAV-HIYIELD account with qualifying new money and maintain the balance to earn a cash bonus.

## Effective Window

- Offer available for accounts opened from 2025-02-01 through 2025-08-31.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must open a new SAV-HIYIELD account.
- Funds must be new money not held at the institution in the prior 90 days.
- Balance must be maintained for the qualification period.
- Existing SAV-HIYIELD holders in the prior 12 months are not eligible.

## Terms Table

| New Money Deposited | Bonus | Balance Hold |
|---------------------|-------|--------------|
| $10,000 – $24,999 | $150 | 90 days |
| $25,000 – $99,999 | $350 | 90 days |
| $100,000+ | $750 | 120 days |

## Disclosures

The bonus is reportable interest. Early withdrawal below the threshold forfeits the bonus. APY is variable and separate from this offer. See required deposit disclosures.

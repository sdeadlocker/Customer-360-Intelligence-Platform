# Mortgage Extended Rate Lock Offer

**Product:** 30 Year Fixed Mortgage (MTG-30F)

## Overview

This document sets out the terms and conditions of the extended rate lock offer for the 30 Year Fixed Mortgage (MTG-30F), allowing qualified purchase applicants to lock a rate for a longer period.

## Offer Summary

Qualified purchase applicants may lock their rate for up to 90 days at a reduced lock cost, protecting against rate movement while under contract.

## Effective Window

- Offer available for locks requested from 2025-03-01 through 2025-09-30.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must have a purchase contract on an eligible property.
- Applicant must be pre-approved for MTG-30F.
- Loan must be expected to close within the lock period.

## Terms Table

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Lock period | Up to 90 days |
| Lock cost | 0.15 points |
| One-time float-down | Available if rates fall |
| Extension fee | 0.05 points per 15 days |

## Disclosures

Rate locks expire; loans not closed within the lock period may require an extension or re-lock at prevailing rates. See TILA lending disclosures and the lending policy.

# New Checking Account Bonus Offer

**Product:** Everyday Checking (DDA-EVERYDAY)

## Overview

This document sets out the terms and conditions of the new-account cash bonus for opening an Everyday Checking (DDA-EVERYDAY) account with qualifying direct deposit.

## Offer Summary

Open a new Everyday Checking account and receive a cash bonus after establishing qualifying direct deposits.

## Effective Window

- Offer available for accounts opened from 2025-03-01 through 2025-10-31.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must open a new DDA-EVERYDAY account.
- Applicant must not have held a checking account in the prior 12 months.
- Qualifying direct deposits totaling $2,000 within 90 days.

## Terms Table

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Qualifying direct deposit | $2,000 within 90 days |
| Bonus amount | $250 |
| Bonus payout | Within 30 days of qualification |
| Account minimum tenure | 180 days |

## Disclosures

The bonus is reportable income. Closing the account before 180 days may result in bonus reversal. See required deposit disclosures.

# Certificate Special Rate Offer

**Product:** 12 Month Certificate (CD-12M)

## Overview

This document sets out the terms and conditions of the promotional special rate on the 12 Month Certificate (CD-12M).

## Offer Summary

Open a new 12 Month Certificate with qualifying funds to earn a promotional APY above the standard certificate rate.

## Effective Window

- Offer available for certificates opened from 2025-04-01 through 2025-07-31.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must open a new CD-12M.
- Minimum deposit of $5,000 in new money.
- Funds not held at the institution in the prior 30 days.

## Terms Table

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Promotional APY | 4.75% |
| Term | 12 months |
| Minimum deposit | $5,000 |
| Early withdrawal penalty | 90 days interest |

## Disclosures

At maturity, the certificate renews at the standard rate then in effect unless instructed otherwise. Early withdrawal penalties may reduce principal. See required deposit disclosures.

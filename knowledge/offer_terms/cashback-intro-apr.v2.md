# Cashback Plus Card Introductory APR Offer (v2 — Current)

**Status:** Current
**Product:** Cashback Plus Card (CC-CASHBACK)

## Overview

This document sets out the current terms and conditions of the introductory purchase APR promotion for the Cashback Plus Card (CC-CASHBACK). Version 2 extends the introductory period and adds a balance transfer offer, superseding the prior terms.

## Offer Summary

New Cashback Plus Card accounts receive a 0% introductory purchase APR for the first 15 billing cycles, plus a 0% introductory balance transfer APR for the first 12 billing cycles. Standard variable APRs apply thereafter.

## Effective Window

- Offer available for applications received from 2024-07-01 through 2025-12-31.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be approved for a new Cashback Plus Card account.
- Applicant must not have held a Cashback Plus Card in the prior 24 months.
- Applicant must meet standard card underwriting criteria.

## Terms Table

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Intro purchase APR | 0% |
| Intro purchase period | 15 billing cycles |
| Intro balance transfer APR | 0% for 12 cycles |
| Balance transfer fee | 3% of amount |
| Standard purchase APR | 18.99%–26.99% variable |

## Disclosures

The introductory rate does not apply to cash advances. This version supersedes v1. Missed payments may end the promotional rate per the cardholder agreement. See required consumer credit disclosures.

# Home Equity Line Promotional Rate Offer

**Product:** Home Equity Line (HELOC-VAR)

## Overview

This document sets out the terms and conditions of the promotional introductory rate for new Home Equity Line (HELOC-VAR) accounts.

## Offer Summary

New HELOC-VAR accounts receive a fixed promotional rate for the first 12 months, after which the standard variable rate (Prime plus margin) applies.

## Effective Window

- Offer available for applications received from 2025-01-01 through 2025-09-30.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be approved for a new HELOC-VAR line.
- Property must be an owner-occupied primary residence.
- Combined loan-to-value must not exceed 85%.
- Minimum initial draw of $25,000 at closing.

## Terms Table

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Promotional rate | 4.99% fixed |
| Promotional period | 12 months |
| Standard rate | Prime + 1.00% variable |
| Annual fee | Waived year one |
| Minimum initial draw | $25,000 |

## Disclosures

Your home secures the line and is at risk in default. After the promotional period, the rate is variable and may increase. Early closure fees may apply. See TILA lending disclosures.

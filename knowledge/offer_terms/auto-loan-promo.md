# New Vehicle Loan Rate Promotion

**Product:** New Vehicle Loan (AUTO-NEW)

## Overview

This document sets out the terms and conditions of the promotional rate on New Vehicle Loans (AUTO-NEW) for qualified applicants.

## Offer Summary

Qualified applicants receive a promotional APR on 60-month new vehicle financing with automatic payment from a linked deposit account.

## Effective Window

- Offer available for applications received from 2025-05-01 through 2025-08-31.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be approved for a new AUTO-NEW loan.
- Minimum credit score of 720 for the promotional tier.
- Automatic payment from a linked deposit account required.
- Vehicle must be current or prior model year, new.

## Terms Table

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Promotional APR | 5.49% |
| Term | 60 months |
| Autopay requirement | Yes |
| Maximum LTV | 120% |

## Disclosures

The promotional rate requires autopay in good standing; loss of autopay may increase the rate per the agreement. See TILA lending disclosures.

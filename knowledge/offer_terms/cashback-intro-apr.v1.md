# Cashback Plus Card Introductory APR Offer (v1 — Superseded)

**Status:** Superseded
**Product:** Cashback Plus Card (CC-CASHBACK)

## Overview

This document sets out the terms and conditions of the introductory purchase APR promotion for the Cashback Plus Card (CC-CASHBACK). This version (v1) applied to accounts opened during the earlier promotional window and has been superseded by updated terms.

## Offer Summary

New Cashback Plus Card accounts receive a 0% introductory purchase APR for the first 12 billing cycles, after which the standard variable purchase APR applies.

## Effective Window

- Offer available for applications received from 2024-01-01 through 2024-06-30.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be approved for a new Cashback Plus Card account.
- Applicant must not have held a Cashback Plus Card in the prior 24 months.
- Applicant must meet standard card underwriting criteria.

## Terms Table

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Intro purchase APR | 0% |
| Intro period | 12 billing cycles |
| Standard purchase APR | 18.99%–26.99% variable |
| Balance transfer | Not included in this offer |

## Disclosures

The introductory rate does not apply to cash advances. Losing the promotional rate for missed payments is governed by the cardholder agreement. See required consumer credit disclosures.

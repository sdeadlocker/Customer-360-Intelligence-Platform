# Required Consumer Disclosures

**Access:** Compliance personnel only

## Overview

This document specifies the disclosure language and delivery requirements that must accompany consumer products at defined trigger points. It ensures customers receive accurate, timely information.

## Scope

- Applies to deposit, lending, and card products across consumer lines.

## Trigger Points

| Event | Required Disclosure |
|-------|---------------------|
| Account opening | Terms, fee schedule, APY/APR |
| Rate change | Advance notice where required |
| Adverse action | Specific reasons for denial |
| Fee change | Advance notice where required |

## Eligibility / Applicability

- Disclosures apply to all covered consumer products.
- Delivery must use a method the customer can retain.

## Required Language Themes

- Deposit accounts must state that APYs are variable where applicable.
- Investment products must state they are not FDIC insured and may lose value.
- Lending products must state APR as a yearly cost of credit.

## Delivery Standards

Disclosures must be clear, conspicuous, and delivered before or at the triggering event. Retain proof of delivery per the retention schedule.

## Recordkeeping

Maintain versioned disclosure templates and delivery evidence for the required period.

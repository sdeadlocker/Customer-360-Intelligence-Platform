# Advice Suitability and Investment Restrictions

**Access:** Compliance personnel only

## Overview

This document defines suitability requirements and restrictions on investment advice, ensuring recommendations are appropriate for the customer's profile.

## Scope

- Applies to investment products including INV-MANAGED, INV-IRA, INV-TRUST, and INV-529.

## Suitability Requirements

- A completed suitability and risk-tolerance assessment is required before recommending investments.
- Recommendations must align with the customer's goals, horizon, and risk tolerance.
- Concentration and liquidity must be considered.

## Eligibility / Applicability

- Applies to all licensed advisors making recommendations.
- Applies at initial recommendation and material profile changes.

## Restricted Statements

| Prohibited | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| Guaranteeing returns | Misleading |
| Omitting risk of loss | Incomplete |
| "FDIC insured" for investments | Inaccurate |

## Required Language Themes

Investment communications must state that investments are not deposits, are not FDIC insured, and may lose value.

## Supervision

Recommendations are subject to supervisory review. Exceptions and complaints route to compliance.

## Recordkeeping

Retain suitability assessments and recommendation rationale per the retention schedule.

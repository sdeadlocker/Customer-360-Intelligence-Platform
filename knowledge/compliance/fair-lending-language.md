# Fair Lending Required Language

**Access:** Compliance personnel only

## Overview

This document defines required fair-lending language and prohibited practices for credit marketing, underwriting, and servicing communications.

## Scope

- Applies to all consumer credit communications and decisions.

## Prohibited-Basis Reminder

Credit decisions and communications must never reference or rely on a prohibited basis, including race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age (with capacity to contract), or receipt of public assistance income.

## Required Language Themes

| Communication | Required Theme |
|---------------|---------------|
| Marketing | Equal availability of credit |
| Adverse action | Specific, accurate reasons |
| Servicing | Consistent, non-discriminatory treatment |

## Eligibility / Applicability

- Applies to every stage of the credit lifecycle and all covered products.

## Adverse Action Standards

Adverse action notices must provide specific reasons for denial, a statement of applicant rights, and the relevant contact for the reporting source used.

## Monitoring and Escalation

Communications and decisions are monitored for disparate treatment and impact. Concerns route to compliance for review.

## Recordkeeping

Retain adverse action notices and monitoring results per the retention schedule.

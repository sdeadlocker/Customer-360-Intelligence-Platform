# Lending Disclosure Requirements (TILA-Style)

**Access:** Compliance personnel only

## Overview

This document specifies the required cost-of-credit disclosures for lending products, ensuring customers can compare offers and understand their obligations.

## Scope

- Applies to MTG-30F, MTG-15F, AUTO-NEW, AUTO-USED, PL-UNSEC, HELOC-VAR, STU-CONS, and BUS-TERM (consumer-purpose).

## Required Elements

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|------------|
| APR | Disclosed as a yearly rate |
| Finance charge | Total cost of credit |
| Amount financed | Disclosed |
| Payment schedule | Number and amount of payments |

## Eligibility / Applicability

- Applies to covered consumer-purpose credit at application and closing.

## Required Language Themes

- APR must be described as the cost of credit expressed as a yearly rate.
- Variable-rate products must disclose how and when the rate may change.
- Secured products must state the security interest and the risk to collateral.

## Adverse Action

Denials must include specific, accurate reasons and notice of applicant rights.

## Recordkeeping

Retain disclosure versions and delivery evidence per the retention schedule.

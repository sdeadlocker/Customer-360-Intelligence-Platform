# Deposit Account Disclosure Requirements

**Access:** Compliance personnel only

## Overview

This document specifies the deposit-specific disclosure requirements for interest, fees, and terms, ensuring customers understand how their accounts work.

## Scope

- Applies to DDA-EVERYDAY, DDA-PREMIER, SAV-CORE, SAV-HIYIELD, MMA-SELECT, CD-12M, CD-36M, and BUS-CHK.

## Required Elements

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|------------|
| APY | Disclosed at opening and on change |
| Fees | Itemized schedule provided |
| Balance methods | Explained clearly |
| Transaction limits | Disclosed where applicable |

## Eligibility / Applicability

- Applies to all covered deposit accounts at opening and on material change.

## Interest Disclosure

Interest disclosures must state whether the rate is fixed or variable, how interest is compounded and credited, and any minimum balance to earn the stated APY.

## Fee Disclosure

Fee schedules must itemize maintenance, overdraft, and transaction fees, and describe conditions for waivers.

## Certificate Disclosure

Certificate disclosures must state the term, maturity handling, and early withdrawal penalties.

## Recordkeeping

Retain disclosure versions and delivery evidence per the retention schedule.

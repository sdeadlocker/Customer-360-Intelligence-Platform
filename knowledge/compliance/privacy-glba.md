# Privacy and Information Sharing (GLBA-Style)

**Access:** Compliance personnel only

## Overview

This document defines privacy notice requirements and limits on sharing customer nonpublic personal information, ensuring customers understand and can limit certain sharing.

## Scope

- Applies to all consumer customers across product lines.

## Required Notices

| Notice | Timing |
|--------|--------|
| Initial privacy notice | At relationship start |
| Annual notice | Where required |
| Opt-out notice | Before certain sharing |

## Eligibility / Applicability

- Applies to nonpublic personal information collected from consumers.

## Sharing Limits

Sharing of nonpublic personal information with nonaffiliated third parties for marketing is subject to the customer's opt-out rights. Certain operational sharing is permitted without opt-out.

## Required Language Themes

Privacy notices must describe what information is collected, how it is used and shared, and how the customer can opt out where applicable.

## Safeguards

Customer information must be protected with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. Access is limited to a need-to-know basis.

## Recordkeeping

Retain privacy notices and opt-out elections per the retention schedule.

# Advisor Playbook: Retirement Planning Conversation

**Audience:** Advisors and relationship bankers

## Overview

This playbook guides a retirement planning conversation, helping advisors uncover goals, assess readiness, and recommend suitable products such as managed investments (INV-MANAGED) and retirement accounts (INV-IRA).

## When to Use

- Customer mentions retirement, rollover, or long-term saving.
- Customer has accumulated deposit balances beyond emergency reserves.
- Annual relationship review for mass-affluent households.

## Eligibility Signals

- Age and time horizon suggest a multi-decade or near-retirement plan.
- Investable assets meet the INV-MANAGED minimum ($50,000).
- Customer has completed or will complete a suitability assessment.

## Conversation Guide

### Discover
- What does an ideal retirement look like for you?
- When would you like the option to retire?
- What retirement accounts do you hold elsewhere?

### Assess
- Review current savings rate and existing balances.
- Discuss risk tolerance and time horizon.
- Identify gaps against the stated goal.

### Recommend
- Propose INV-MANAGED aligned to the risk profile.
- Discuss IRA contributions or rollovers (INV-IRA).
- Position Premier Checking (DDA-PREMIER) for relationship benefits.

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response Theme |
|-----------|---------------|
| "I already have a 401(k)" | Consolidation and holistic planning |
| "Markets feel risky" | Time horizon and diversification |
| "I'll start later" | Cost of delay illustration |

## Compliance Note

Recommendations must meet suitability requirements. Investments are not FDIC insured and may lose value. See advice suitability compliance guidance.

# Advisor Playbook: Business Formation Conversation

**Audience:** Business bankers

## Overview

This playbook guides a conversation with a customer forming a new business, positioning the Business Operating Account (BUS-CHK) and related products.

## When to Use

- Customer is starting or has recently registered a business.
- Customer needs to separate business and personal finances.
- Customer asks about business banking or lending.

## Eligibility Signals

- Registered entity with a tax identification number.
- Beneficial ownership can be certified.
- Authorized signer with valid identification.

## Conversation Guide

### Discover
- What type of business are you forming?
- How will you handle payroll and vendor payments?
- Do you anticipate needing financing?

### Recommend
- Open a Business Operating Account (BUS-CHK).
- Position the Business Expense Card (CC-BUSINESS) for spend control.
- Introduce the Business Term Loan (BUS-TERM) for capital needs.

## Onboarding Checklist

| Item | Required |
|------|----------|
| Entity registration | Yes |
| Tax ID | Yes |
| Beneficial ownership | Yes |
| Authorized signer ID | Yes |

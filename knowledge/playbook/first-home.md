# Advisor Playbook: First-Time Homebuyer Conversation

**Audience:** Mortgage advisors and bankers

## Overview

This playbook guides a first-time homebuyer conversation, helping advisors educate the customer and position the 30 Year Fixed Mortgage (MTG-30F) with appropriate down-payment options.

## When to Use

- Customer expresses interest in buying a first home.
- Customer is saving toward a down payment.
- Customer asks about pre-approval.

## Eligibility Signals

- Credit score meets or approaches the MTG-30F minimum (620).
- Documented, stable income.
- Down payment of at least 3% for qualified first-time buyers.

## Conversation Guide

### Discover
- What price range are you considering?
- How much have you saved for a down payment?
- What is your timeline to purchase?

### Educate
- Explain pre-approval versus pre-qualification.
- Walk through closing costs and escrow.
- Discuss the importance of debt-to-income.

### Recommend
- Position MTG-30F with a suitable down-payment option.
- Set up a dedicated savings goal in Core Savings (SAV-CORE).
- Offer pre-approval to strengthen offers.

## Readiness Checklist

| Item | Status |
|------|--------|
| Credit reviewed | Yes/No |
| Down payment saved | Amount |
| Income documented | Yes/No |
| Pre-approval issued | Yes/No |

## Compliance Note

Provide accurate cost and rate estimates. See TILA lending disclosures and the lending policy.

# Advisor Playbook: Mortgage Refinance Conversation

**Audience:** Mortgage advisors and bankers

## Overview

This playbook guides a refinance conversation, helping advisors determine whether refinancing benefits the customer and positioning the right product (MTG-30F or MTG-15F).

## When to Use

- Customer holds an existing mortgage elsewhere or with the institution.
- Rates or the customer's situation have changed materially.
- Customer wants to shorten term or access equity.

## Eligibility Signals

- Sufficient equity and qualifying credit for a new mortgage.
- Break-even analysis favors refinancing.
- Debt-to-income within lending policy thresholds.

## Conversation Guide

### Discover
- What is your current rate, balance, and remaining term?
- What are you hoping to achieve by refinancing?
- Are you planning to stay in the home long term?

### Assess
- Estimate closing costs and monthly savings.
- Calculate the break-even period.
- Discuss term change (30-year to 15-year) tradeoffs.

### Recommend
- Position MTG-30F for payment relief or MTG-15F for faster payoff.
- Introduce HELOC-VAR if equity access is the goal.

## Break-Even Illustration

| Factor | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Monthly savings | $180 |
| Closing costs | $4,500 |
| Break-even | 25 months |

## Compliance Note

Provide accurate APR and cost estimates. See TILA lending disclosures and the lending policy.

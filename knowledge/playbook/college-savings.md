# Advisor Playbook: College Savings Conversation

**Audience:** Advisors and relationship bankers

## Overview

This playbook guides a college savings conversation, helping advisors position education savings accounts (INV-529) and complementary deposit products.

## When to Use

- Customer mentions saving for a child's education.
- Customer has young dependents and available savings capacity.
- Annual relationship review with families.

## Eligibility Signals

- A beneficiary can be named for an education account.
- Customer has savings capacity beyond emergency reserves.
- Time horizon supports an investment approach.

## Conversation Guide

### Discover
- Who are you saving for and how old are they?
- What portion of costs do you hope to cover?
- Are grandparents or others contributing?

### Recommend
- Position an education savings account (INV-529).
- Set up recurring contributions from Core Savings (SAV-CORE).
- Revisit allocation as the beneficiary approaches college age.

# Advisor Playbook: Delinquency Outreach Conversation

**Audience:** Servicing and collections staff

## Overview

This playbook guides respectful, compliant outreach to customers who have fallen behind on payments, focusing on understanding the situation and offering suitable options.

## When to Use

- Account is past due beyond the grace period.
- Delinquency treatment procedure has flagged the account for outreach.

## Eligibility Signals

- Customer is reachable through permitted channels.
- Hardship program eligibility may apply.

## Conversation Guide

### Open with Empathy
- Acknowledge the situation without judgment.
- Confirm you are speaking with the right person and follow verification.

### Understand
- What changed that made payments difficult?
- Is this a temporary or ongoing situation?

### Offer Options
- Present repayment or hardship options the customer qualifies for.
- Explain the impact of each option clearly.

### Confirm
- Summarize the agreed arrangement.
- Set expectations for next steps and follow-up.

## Options Matrix

| Situation | Option |
|-----------|--------|
| Temporary hardship | Short-term deferral |
| Reduced income | Repayment plan |
| Long-term hardship | Loss mitigation review |

## Compliance Note

Follow permitted contact rules and treat customers consistently. See the delinquency treatment procedure and fair-lending compliance guidance.

# Advisor Playbook: Affluent Cross-Sell Conversation

**Audience:** Relationship bankers and advisors

## Overview

This playbook guides a holistic relationship review with affluent and mass-affluent customers, deepening the relationship across deposits, cards, lending, and wealth.

## When to Use

- Customer qualifies for Premier Checking (DDA-PREMIER).
- Combined balances suggest wealth or premium-card fit.
- Annual relationship review.

## Eligibility Signals

- Combined relationship balances of $25,000+ (Premier) or $100,000+ (Private Client).
- Investable assets meeting the INV-MANAGED minimum.
- Interest in travel or premium benefits.

## Conversation Guide

### Discover
- How are your accounts organized across institutions?
- What are your priorities: growth, income, or convenience?
- How do you prefer to be served?

### Recommend
- Consolidate into Premier Checking (DDA-PREMIER).
- Position INV-MANAGED for long-horizon assets.
- Offer the Private Client Card (CC-PRIVATE) or Travel Elite Card (CC-TRAVEL).
- Introduce HELOC-VAR for flexible liquidity.

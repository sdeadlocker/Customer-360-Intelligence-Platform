# Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) Filing Procedure

**Access:** Risk personnel only

## Overview

This procedure defines the steps for evaluating, drafting, and filing a Suspicious Activity Report when activity warrants. It ensures deadlines and confidentiality requirements are met.

## Eligibility to File

- Only AML investigations staff may draft and file a SAR.
- The filing decision is made by the designated SAR committee or officer.

## Procedure Steps

1. Receive the escalation from the AML investigations queue.
2. Gather supporting documentation, transaction history, and prior cases.
3. Analyze whether the activity is suspicious and lacks a lawful explanation.
4. Document the five essential elements: who, what, when, where, and why.
5. Present findings to the SAR committee for a filing decision.
6. If filing, complete and submit the SAR within the regulatory deadline.
7. Retain the SAR and all supporting documentation per the retention schedule.
8. Continue monitoring; file continuing-activity SARs as required.

## Deadlines

| Event | Deadline |
|-------|----------|
| Initial SAR | 30 calendar days from detection |
| Extension (no suspect) | Up to 60 calendar days |
| Continuing activity | Every 90 days if ongoing |

## Confidentiality

SAR existence and content are strictly confidential. Disclosing a SAR to the subject is prohibited by law.

## Recordkeeping

Retain SAR filings and support for the required retention period.

# Card Fraud Block and Reissue Procedure

**Access:** Risk personnel only

## Overview

This procedure defines how to block a compromised card, reissue a replacement, and manage recurring payments during the transition.

## Eligibility to Act

- Fraud or card operations staff execute blocks and reissues.
- The customer must be authenticated before any action.

## Procedure Steps

1. Authenticate the cardholder using approved methods.
2. Confirm the compromise indicators (lost, stolen, or unauthorized use).
3. Place an immediate block on the card number.
4. Order a reissue with a new card number and CVV.
5. Identify recurring merchant payments on the old card.
6. Advise the customer on updating recurring payments.
7. Open a dispute for any confirmed unauthorized transactions.
8. Document the case and close upon resolution.

## Block Types

| Block Type | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Temporary lock | Reversible by customer |
| Fraud block | Permanent, requires reissue |
| Lost/stolen | Permanent, requires reissue |

## Recordkeeping

Retain the fraud case and reissue record per the retention schedule.

## Confidentiality

Share only the information necessary for the customer to protect their account.

# Delinquency Treatment Procedure

**Access:** Risk personnel only

## Overview

This procedure defines the servicing steps applied as a loan or card account becomes progressively delinquent, from early-stage outreach through charge-off referral.

## Eligibility for Treatment

- Applies to accounts past the payment due date beyond any grace period.
- Hardship programs require documented eligibility.

## Procedure Steps

1. Identify the account entering delinquency after the grace period.
2. Attempt early-stage contact through permitted channels.
3. Offer hardship or repayment options where the customer qualifies.
4. Escalate treatment intensity as days-past-due increase.
5. Issue required notices before adverse actions.
6. Refer to charge-off and collections at the defined threshold.
7. Report accurate status to credit reporting agencies.

## Treatment Stages

| Days Past Due | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| 1–15 | Reminder, grace handling |
| 16–59 | Outreach, hardship review |
| 60–89 | Escalated outreach, notices |
| 90+ | Charge-off referral |

## Recordkeeping

Retain contact logs, notices, and arrangements per the retention schedule.

## Fair Servicing

Treatment must be applied consistently. See fair-lending compliance guidance and the delinquency outreach playbook.

# Transaction Dispute Resolution Procedure

**Access:** Risk personnel only

## Overview

This procedure defines how customer disputes of card and ACH transactions are received, investigated, and resolved within regulatory timelines.

## Eligibility to File a Dispute

- The customer must be an owner or authorized user of the account.
- The disputed transaction must be identified with date and amount.

## Procedure Steps

1. Authenticate the customer and record the dispute details.
2. Classify the dispute type (unauthorized, billing error, or service dispute).
3. Provide provisional credit where required within the applicable timeframe.
4. Open a chargeback or ACH return as appropriate.
5. Gather evidence from the customer and merchant or originator.
6. Adjudicate the dispute based on network or ACH rules.
7. Notify the customer of the outcome in writing.
8. Reverse provisional credit if the dispute is denied, with notice.

## Timelines

| Dispute Type | Provisional Credit | Resolution |
|--------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Card unauthorized | Within 10 business days | Up to 90 days |
| Billing error | Within 10 business days | Two cycles |
| ACH unauthorized | Next business day | Per ACH rules |

## Recordkeeping

Retain dispute documentation and outcome per the retention schedule.

## Disclosures

Customers are informed of their rights and the investigation timeline at intake.

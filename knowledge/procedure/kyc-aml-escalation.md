# KYC/AML Escalation Procedure

**Access:** Risk personnel only

## Overview

This procedure defines the steps for escalating potential anti-money-laundering (AML) concerns identified during onboarding or ongoing monitoring. It ensures timely, documented escalation to the AML team.

## Eligibility to Escalate

- Any employee may raise a concern; formal escalation is handled by risk-designated staff.
- Escalators must not tip off the customer.

## Procedure Steps

1. Identify the trigger (unusual activity, adverse media, sanctions hit, or CIP discrepancy).
2. Preserve all relevant records and transaction detail.
3. Document the concern in the case management system with objective facts only.
4. Assign an initial risk rating using the KYC onboarding risk matrix.
5. Escalate to the AML investigations queue within one business day.
6. For confirmed sanctions matches, freeze activity and notify the sanctions officer immediately.
7. Await disposition; do not close the customer relationship unilaterally.

## Risk Triggers

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Sanctions list hit | Immediate freeze + sanctions officer |
| Structuring pattern | Escalate to AML within 1 day |
| Adverse media | Enhanced due diligence review |
| PEP identified | Route to PEP review procedure |

## Recordkeeping

All escalations and dispositions are retained per the AML retention schedule.

## Confidentiality

Escalation and investigation details are confidential. Tipping off a customer is prohibited and may violate law.

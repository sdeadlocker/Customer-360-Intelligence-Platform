# Fraud Handling Procedure

**Access:** Risk personnel only

## Overview

This procedure defines the steps to triage, contain, and resolve suspected account fraud, including unauthorized transactions and account takeover.

## Eligibility to Act

- Fraud operations staff execute containment steps.
- Front-line staff initiate the case and verify the customer.

## Procedure Steps

1. Verify the customer's identity using approved authentication methods.
2. Confirm the reported unauthorized activity and capture details.
3. Place a hold or block on the affected instrument or account.
4. Open a fraud case with timeline and affected transactions.
5. Issue replacement cards or credentials as needed.
6. File provisional credit where required by dispute rules.
7. Refer to AML escalation if the pattern suggests money laundering.
8. Resolve the case and document the final determination.

## Containment Matrix

| Fraud Type | Immediate Action |
|-----------|------------------|
| Card compromise | Block card, reissue |
| Account takeover | Reset credentials, lock account |
| Check fraud | Stop payment, hold deposits |
| Wire fraud | Attempt recall, notify beneficiary bank |

## Recordkeeping

Retain fraud case records per the retention schedule.

## Confidentiality

Do not disclose investigative methods to the customer beyond what is necessary to resolve the case.

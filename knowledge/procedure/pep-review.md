# Politically Exposed Person (PEP) Review Procedure

**Access:** Risk personnel only

## Overview

This procedure defines enhanced due diligence for customers identified as politically exposed persons (PEPs) or their close associates, ensuring elevated risk is appropriately managed.

## Eligibility / Trigger

- Triggered when screening or customer disclosure identifies PEP status.
- Applies at onboarding and on periodic review.

## Procedure Steps

1. Confirm the PEP determination and category (domestic, foreign, or associate).
2. Gather source-of-wealth and source-of-funds information.
3. Assess the customer's risk in the context of products and geography.
4. Obtain senior management approval before activation or continuation.
5. Apply enhanced ongoing monitoring to the relationship.
6. Schedule periodic re-review at a heightened frequency.
7. Escalate any adverse findings to AML investigations.

## Risk Considerations

| Factor | Consideration |
|--------|--------------|
| Category | Foreign PEPs carry higher inherent risk |
| Source of wealth | Must be plausible and documented |
| Products | Wires and investments increase scrutiny |
| Adverse media | Requires additional review |

## Recordkeeping

Retain approvals, source-of-wealth documentation, and review notes per the retention schedule.

## Confidentiality

PEP reviews are confidential. Do not disclose the review to the customer beyond necessary due-diligence questions.

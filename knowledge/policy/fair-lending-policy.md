# Fair Lending Policy

**Applies to:** All credit products and decisions

## Overview

This policy affirms the institution's commitment to fair and equitable access to credit and prohibits discrimination on any prohibited basis. It defines expectations for underwriting, pricing, marketing, and servicing.

## Scope

- Applies to all lending and card products and to every stage of the credit lifecycle.

## Prohibited-Basis Factors

Credit decisions must never be influenced by race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age (provided the applicant can contract), receipt of public assistance income, or the good-faith exercise of consumer credit rights.

## Eligibility and Consistency

- Underwriting criteria must be applied consistently to all applicants.
- Pricing must be based on documented, risk-based factors only.
- Exceptions must be documented and monitored for disparate impact.

## Monitoring

| Area | Control |
|------|---------|
| Underwriting | Consistency testing across cohorts |
| Pricing | Rate-exception review |
| Marketing | Prescreen criteria review |
| Servicing | Delinquency treatment consistency |

## Complaint Handling

Fair-lending complaints must be escalated to compliance for tracking and root-cause review.

## Disclosures

Adverse action notices must state specific, accurate reasons. See required lending disclosures and fair-lending compliance guidance.

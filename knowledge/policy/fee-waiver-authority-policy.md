# Fee Waiver Authority Policy

**Applies to:** Deposit and card fee waivers

## Overview

This policy defines who may waive fees, the limits on waiver authority, and the documentation required. It ensures fee waivers are used for legitimate service recovery rather than routine practice.

## Scope

- Applies to overdraft, maintenance, ATM, and card fees across consumer products.

## Eligibility to Waive

- Waiver authority requires an active servicing role designation.
- Bankers may not waive fees on their own accounts or related-party accounts.
- Repeated waivers to the same customer require supervisor review.

## Authority Limits

| Role | Per-Waiver Limit | Monthly Cap |
|------|-----------------|-------------|
| Contact Center Agent | $35 | $105 |
| Banker | $75 | $300 |
| Branch Manager | $250 | $1,500 |
| Regional Manager | $1,000 | No cap (logged) |

## Documentation

Every waiver must record the reason code, amount, and approver. Waivers are subject to periodic audit for pattern and abuse detection.

## Escalation

Requests above an approver's limit escalate to the next authority level. Splitting a waiver to stay under limits is prohibited.

## Disclosures

This policy operates alongside the deposit account policy and overdraft policy.

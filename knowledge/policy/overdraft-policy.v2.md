# Overdraft Policy (v2 — Current)

**Status:** Current
**Applies to:** Consumer deposit accounts

## Overview

This policy governs how the institution handles transactions that would overdraw a consumer deposit account. Version 2 reduces overdraft fees, introduces a grace amount, and caps daily fees, superseding the prior version. It defines when overdrafts may be paid, the fees that apply, and the customer's options.

## Scope

- Applies to Everyday Checking (DDA-EVERYDAY), Premier Checking (DDA-PREMIER), and Core Savings (SAV-CORE) when configured for overdraft.
- Covers debit card, ACH, and check transactions.

## Overdraft Handling

- Everyday debit card and ATM overdrafts require affirmative opt-in.
- ACH and check items may be paid into overdraft at the institution's discretion.
- Transactions overdrawing the account by $10 or less are not charged a fee (grace amount).
- Items are processed low-to-high within each business day to reduce fee incidence.

## Fee Schedule

| Item | Amount | Limit |
|------|--------|-------|
| Overdraft item fee | $30.00 | Up to 3 per day |
| Grace amount (no fee) | $10.00 | Per day |
| Returned item fee | $0.00 | Eliminated |
| Extended overdraft fee | $0.00 | Eliminated |

## Eligibility

- Account must be open at least 30 days for discretionary overdraft coverage.
- Account must not be subject to a collections hold.
- Customer must not have a negative balance older than 30 days.

## Customer Options

Customers may opt out of debit card overdraft coverage at any time and may link a Core Savings account for overdraft protection transfers at no cost.

## Disclosures

Fees are assessed per the schedule above and disclosed at account opening. This version supersedes v1. See required consumer disclosures.

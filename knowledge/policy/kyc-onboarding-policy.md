# KYC Onboarding Policy

**Applies to:** All new customer relationships

## Overview

This policy defines the Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements for onboarding new consumer and business relationships. It establishes the customer identification program (CIP), beneficial ownership requirements, and risk rating at onboarding.

## Scope

- Applies to all new deposit, lending, card, and investment relationships.

## Customer Identification Program

- Collect legal name, date of birth, address, and identification number.
- Verify identity through documentary or non-documentary methods.
- Screen against sanctions and prohibited-party lists.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must provide valid, unexpired identification.
- Applicant must not appear on any sanctions or prohibited list.
- Business applicants must certify beneficial ownership for owners of 25%+.

## Risk Rating

| Factor | Lower Risk | Higher Risk |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Customer type | Salaried consumer | Cash-intensive business |
| Geography | Domestic | High-risk jurisdiction |
| Product | Checking/savings | International wires |
| PEP status | No | Yes |

## Enhanced Due Diligence

Higher-risk customers, including politically exposed persons (PEPs), require enhanced due diligence and senior approval before activation. See the PEP review procedure.

## Recordkeeping

Identification and verification records must be retained per the applicable retention schedule.

## Disclosures

This policy supports AML program requirements. See the KYC/AML escalation procedure.

# Dormant and Inactive Account Policy

**Applies to:** Consumer and business deposit accounts

## Overview

This policy defines how accounts are classified as inactive or dormant, the controls applied to dormant accounts, and the escheatment process for unclaimed property.

## Scope

- Applies to DDA-EVERYDAY, DDA-PREMIER, SAV-CORE, SAV-HIYIELD, MMA-SELECT, and BUS-CHK.

## Classification

| Status | Definition |
|--------|-----------|
| Active | Customer-initiated activity within 12 months |
| Inactive | No customer activity for 12–24 months |
| Dormant | No customer activity for 24+ months |

## Eligibility for Reactivation

- Account owner must complete identity re-verification.
- Any holds must be cleared before reactivation.
- Reactivation restores standard access and fee treatment.

## Controls on Dormant Accounts

- Enhanced monitoring for unauthorized activity.
- Suppression of certain outbound communications to reduce fraud risk.
- Manual review before large withdrawals.

## Escheatment

Accounts meeting the state-defined abandonment period are reported and remitted as unclaimed property after required owner outreach.

## Disclosures

Owners are notified before dormancy classification where required. See required deposit disclosures.

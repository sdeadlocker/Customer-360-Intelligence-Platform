# Funds Availability Policy

**Applies to:** Consumer and business deposit accounts

## Overview

This policy defines when funds deposited to an account become available for withdrawal. It balances customer access with fraud and returned-item risk.

## Scope

- Applies to DDA-EVERYDAY, DDA-PREMIER, SAV-CORE, MMA-SELECT, and BUS-CHK.

## Availability Schedule

| Deposit Type | Availability |
|--------------|-------------|
| Direct deposit / wire | Same business day |
| Cash (in branch) | Next business day |
| Electronic (ACH) | Next business day |
| Check (standard) | Second business day |
| Large or new-account checks | Up to 7 business days |

## Eligibility for Standard Availability

- Account must be open more than 30 days.
- Account must not have repeated overdrafts.
- Deposit must not trigger a reasonable-cause hold.

## Holds

Holds may be placed for new accounts, large deposits, redeposited items, or reasonable cause to doubt collectibility. Customers are notified when a hold is placed.

## Disclosures

This policy is provided at account opening and on request. See required deposit disclosures.

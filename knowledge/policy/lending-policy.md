# Consumer Lending Policy

**Applies to:** All consumer lending products

## Overview

This policy establishes the underwriting standards, approval criteria, and exception process for consumer lending across mortgage, auto, personal, and home equity products. It ensures consistent, fair, and prudent credit decisions.

## Scope

- Applies to MTG-30F, MTG-15F, AUTO-NEW, AUTO-USED, PL-UNSEC, HELOC-VAR, and STU-CONS.
- Governs both direct and referred originations.

## Underwriting Standards

- Credit score minimums are set per product and may not be waived below floor thresholds.
- Debt-to-income (DTI) ratios must be documented and verified.
- Income and assets must be verified with acceptable documentation.
- Collateral valuations must come from approved third-party sources.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be of legal age and have legal capacity to contract.
- Applicant must pass identity verification and screening.
- Applicant must meet the product-specific credit, DTI, and collateral thresholds.
- Applicant must not be on any prohibited or sanctions list.

## Approval Thresholds

| Product | Min Credit Score | Max DTI | Max LTV/CLTV |
|---------|-----------------|---------|--------------|
| MTG-30F | 620 | 45% | 97% |
| MTG-15F | 640 | 43% | 95% |
| AUTO-NEW | 620 | 45% | 120% |
| PL-UNSEC | 660 | 40% | n/a |
| HELOC-VAR | 680 | 43% | 85% |

## Exception Process

Exceptions to non-floor criteria require documented compensating factors and approval per the credit-limit authority matrix. Exceptions below floor thresholds are prohibited.

## Fair Lending

All decisions must comply with fair-lending requirements. Prohibited-basis factors must never influence credit decisions. See fair-lending compliance guidance.

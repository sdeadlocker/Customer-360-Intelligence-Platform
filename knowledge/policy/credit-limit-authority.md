# Credit Limit and Approval Authority Policy

**Applies to:** Lending and card credit decisions

## Overview

This policy defines the delegated authority levels for approving credit limits, loan amounts, and underwriting exceptions. It ensures decisions are made at an appropriate level of seniority relative to risk.

## Scope

- Applies to consumer and business lending and to credit card limit assignments.

## Eligibility to Exercise Authority

- Approver must hold an active lending authority designation.
- Approver must have completed annual credit training.
- Approver may not approve credit for related parties or their own accounts.

## Authority Matrix

| Role | Max Loan / Limit | Exception Authority |
|------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Banker | $25,000 | None |
| Senior Banker | $100,000 | Non-floor, documented |
| Credit Officer | $500,000 | Non-floor and DTI |
| Credit Committee | Above $500,000 | All permitted exceptions |

## Exception Handling

- Floor-threshold criteria (minimum credit score) may never be waived.
- Non-floor exceptions require documented compensating factors.
- All exceptions must be logged with rationale and approver identity.

## Escalation

Requests exceeding an approver's authority must be escalated to the next level. Split approvals to circumvent authority limits are prohibited.

## Disclosures

This policy operates alongside the consumer lending policy and fair-lending requirements.

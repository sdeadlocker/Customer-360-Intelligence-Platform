# Deposit Account Policy

**Applies to:** All consumer and business deposit accounts

## Overview

This policy defines account opening standards, balance and fee-waiver rules, transaction limits, and relationship-balance calculations for deposit products. It ensures consistent treatment across the deposit portfolio.

## Scope

- Applies to DDA-EVERYDAY, DDA-PREMIER, SAV-CORE, SAV-HIYIELD, MMA-SELECT, CD-12M, CD-36M, and BUS-CHK.

## Account Opening

- All owners must complete identity verification per KYC onboarding policy.
- Minimum opening deposits are enforced per product.
- Deposit screening is required before account activation.

## Eligibility

- Account owner must meet the age and identification requirements for the product.
- Business accounts require beneficial ownership certification.
- Owners must not appear on prohibited or sanctions lists.

## Fee Waivers and Relationship Balances

| Rule | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Relationship balance | Sum of eligible deposit and investment balances by tax ID |
| Direct-deposit waiver | Qualifying recurring credit of $500+ per cycle |
| Balance waiver | Average or minimum daily balance per product |
| Premier qualification | $25,000 combined relationship balance |

## Transaction Limits

Savings and money market accounts may be subject to a 6-per-cycle limit on certain withdrawals; excess transactions incur fees per the product fee schedule.

## Disclosures

APYs are variable unless a fixed-term certificate. Fee schedules are provided at opening and on change. See required deposit disclosures.
